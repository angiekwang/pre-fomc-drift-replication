\caption*{
\textbf{Table B.3. CRSP Size Portfolio Regressions: 1994--2011.}
This table provides results for a FOMC dummy variable regression of different weighted U.S. equity portfolios over the sample period from September 1, 1994 through March 30, 2011. The dependent variable is daily percent log-excess returns, and portfolios include value-weighted and equal weighted portfolios from CRSP as well as the ten decile size portfolios from \href{https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html}{Ken French's online data library}. ``FOMC dummy'' is 1 on days with a FOMC announcement and 0 otherwise. ``Annual ex-return FOMC'' is the average cumulative annual excess return on FOMC days, and ``Annual ex-return non-FOMC'' is the average cumulative annual return on non-FOMC days in the sample. ``FOMC Sharpe Ratio'' is the annualized Sharpe ratio on pre-FOMC returns. Slight differences in values may be due to CRSP revisions and changes in French’s daily portfolio methodology. *** significant at 1\%, ** significant at 5\%, * significant at 10\%. HC1 robust standard errors shown in brackets.
